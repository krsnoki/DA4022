## Objective

Students are encouraged to explore the topics covered in this repository, identify potential improvements, and contribute implementations, experiments, or optimizations that advance the existing work.

## Contribution Guidelines

1. **Explore the assigned topic** by reviewing the relevant folder and understanding its existing implementation.
2. **Read relevant research papers** from the [ICML 2026 AdaptFM Workshop — Accepted Posters](https://openreview.net/group?id=ICML.cc/2026/Workshop/AdaptFM#tab-accept-poster).
3. **Identify an opportunity for improvement** based on the literature, such as improved efficiency, reduced resource consumption, better performance, or a novel approach.
4. **Implement and evaluate your idea** with appropriate baselines and reproducible experiments.
5. **Document your contribution** clearly, including the motivation, implementation, results, and relevant paper references.
6. **Submit a pull request** with your code, documentation, and experimental findings.

## Expected Outcome

The goal is to encourage **innovative, evidence-based contributions** that improve existing methods or introduce promising new ideas. Students should focus on meaningful improvements, reproducibility, and clear documentation rather than implementation alone.

All contributions should reference relevant research, include appropriate evaluations, and follow good open-source development practices.
